Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Bobak Game Studio (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with our game My Book Store (the “Game”). We have designed the Game to collect as little personal data as possible.

1. Who we are

The Game is developed and operated by Bobak Game Studio. For any privacy question or request you can contact us at bobakgamestudio@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

We do not ask you for your name, email, phone number, or any account registration to play. The data involved is:

DataPurposeBasis
Anonymous gameplay analytics — progress through days, quests and locations, and in-game shop purchases (in-game currency only). To understand whether players get through the early game, how the daily shop loop performs, and where progression stalls, so we can improve the Game. Collected only after you consent on the first-run screen. You can decline, and you may change your mind — see “Your choices”.
Save data — your in-game progress, tied to a randomly generated player identifier (player_id) created on your device. Not linked to your real-world identity. To store your progress and let you continue where you left off. Necessary to provide the Game’s save functionality.

We do not collect your device’s advertising identifier (Advertising ID), and crash reporting (Crashlytics) is switched off. The analytics we collect are aggregated and anonymous and are not used to identify you personally.

3. Third parties we use

We rely on the following service providers, who process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — anonymous gameplay analytics (only after your consent). See Firebase’s privacy information.
  • Google Firebase Remote Config — delivering game configuration and balancing values.
  • Cloudflare R2 — hosting and delivering downloadable game content (assets).
  • Our own game server — storing your save data and serving configuration.

4. Data retention

We keep your save data for as long as it exists on our server, i.e. while you continue to use the Game, and until you ask us to delete it (see below). If you reinstall the Game or clear its data, the local player_id is lost and the associated save can no longer be matched to you. Anonymous analytics are retained in aggregated form according to our Firebase Analytics settings and cannot be traced back to an individual.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Analytics consent. Analytics are collected only if you agree on the first-run consent screen. If you decline, no analytics are sent.
  • Access and deletion. You can ask us what save data we hold for your player_id and ask us to delete it. Email bobakgamestudio@gmail.com and include your player_id so we can locate the data. We will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
